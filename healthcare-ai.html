🏥 Healthcare & AI

My professional journey began in healthcare, where I developed experience in maternal and newborn care and gained a strong understanding of the importance of accurate information in healthcare decision-making. I am now combining my healthcare background with Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Artificial Intelligence to explore how technology can support better healthcare insights and decision-making.

👩‍⚕️ Healthcare Background

My healthcare experience gives me a unique perspective when working with healthcare data. I understand that behind every row in a healthcare dataset there is a patient, a clinical decision, or a healthcare outcome. This perspective helps me approach healthcare analytics with both technical curiosity and an understanding of the human side of healthcare.
